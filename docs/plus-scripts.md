# Mindful Moments Plus — Meditation Scripts (v1.3)

Full word-for-word scripts for **Self-Compassion** and **Body Scan** guided meditations.

**Voice:** Dominic (primary) — Mira can use the same scripts with a warmer delivery in v1.3.1  
**Pace:** ~115–125 words per minute, plus pauses marked `[pause]`  
**Recording tip:** Treat `[pause — 15s]` as silent hold time — do not speak it aloud.

# Self-Compassion

---

## 5 minutes

Welcome to this Self-Compassion meditation. Find a comfortable position — sitting or lying down. Let your body be supported. There is nothing you need to fix or achieve in the next few minutes. Simply being here is enough.

When you’re ready, gently close your eyes, or soften your gaze downward.

[pause — 10s]

Begin by noticing your breath. Don’t change it. Just observe. The gentle rise and fall of your chest. The air moving in… and out.

[pause — 15s]

Perhaps today has asked a lot of you. Or perhaps you’ve been carrying something quietly — stress, doubt, tiredness, or a sense that you should be doing more. Whatever is present, you don’t need to push it away. You can let it be here, just for now.

[pause — 15s]

Self-compassion begins with honesty. Noticing what is true, without judging yourself for it. You are human. And being human includes difficult moments.

[pause — 10s]

Now, if it feels comfortable, place one hand over your heart. Feel the warmth of your palm. The steady rhythm beneath. This simple gesture is a reminder: you deserve kindness too.

[pause — 15s]

Silently, repeat these words in your mind. Take your time with each phrase.

*May I be kind to myself in this moment.*

[pause — 12s]

*May I give myself the patience I would offer a good friend.*

[pause — 12s]

*May I remember that I am doing my best with what I have today.*

[pause — 15s]

If critical thoughts arise — and they may — notice them without following them. You don’t need to argue. You can simply return to the phrase: *May I be kind to myself.*

[pause — 20s]

Feel your breath. Steady. Patient. Your body has carried you through every day of your life. That deserves gratitude, not criticism.

[pause — 15s]

Slowly deepen your breath. A little more fully in… and a little more fully out.

[pause — 10s]

When you’re ready, gently open your eyes. Carry this kindness with you — not as something you must earn, but as something you already deserve.

[pause — 5s]

Thank you for showing up for yourself today.

## 10 minutes

Welcome to this Self-Compassion meditation. Find a comfortable position — sitting or lying down. Let your body be supported. There is nothing you need to fix or achieve in the next few minutes. Simply being here is enough.

When you’re ready, gently close your eyes, or soften your gaze downward.

[pause — 10s]

Begin by noticing your breath. Don’t change it. Just observe. The gentle rise and fall of your chest. The air moving in… and out.

[pause — 15s]

Perhaps today has asked a lot of you. Or perhaps you’ve been carrying something quietly — stress, doubt, tiredness, or a sense that you should be doing more. Whatever is present, you don’t need to push it away. You can let it be here, just for now.

[pause — 15s]

Self-compassion begins with honesty. Noticing what is true, without judging yourself for it. You are human. And being human includes difficult moments.

[pause — 10s]

Now, if it feels comfortable, place one hand over your heart. Feel the warmth of your palm. The steady rhythm beneath.

[pause — 15s]

Silently, repeat these words in your mind.

*May I be kind to myself in this moment.*

[pause — 12s]

*May I give myself the patience I would offer a good friend.*

[pause — 12s]

*May I remember that I am doing my best with what I have today.*

[pause — 20s]

Now, bring to mind something that has felt difficult recently. It doesn’t need to be your biggest struggle. Something small is enough. A conversation that stung. A mistake you keep replaying. A moment when you felt you fell short.

[pause — 15s]

Notice where you feel this in your body. Perhaps a tightness in your chest. A heaviness in your stomach. A tension in your shoulders. Just notice. No need to change anything yet.

[pause — 20s]

Whisper inwardly, or think quietly:

*This is a moment of suffering.*

[pause — 10s]

*Suffering is part of life — I am not alone in this.*

[pause — 10s]

*May I be kind to myself in this moment.*

[pause — 20s]

Think of someone you care about — a friend, a family member, someone you love. If they came to you with this same struggle, what would you say to them? Perhaps: *It’s okay. You’re allowed to feel this. I’m here with you.*

[pause — 15s]

Now turn that same warmth toward yourself. You deserve the same gentleness you would offer them. The same patience. The same understanding.

[pause — 20s]

*May I be kind to myself.*

[pause — 15s]

*May I forgive myself for being imperfect.*

[pause — 15s]

*May I treat myself with the care I deserve.*

[pause — 25s]

Rest here in quiet. Nothing to fix. Nothing to prove. Just breath, and kindness, and this moment.

[pause — 30s]

If the inner critic returns, acknowledge it gently: *Thank you for trying to protect me. But right now, I choose compassion.*

[pause — 20s]

Slowly deepen your breath. Feel your body supported. Feel the space you’ve created inside yourself — a little softer than before.

[pause — 15s]

When you’re ready, gently open your eyes. Carry this kindness with you. You don’t have to be perfect to be worthy of care.

[pause — 5s]

Thank you for showing up for yourself today.

## 15 minutes

Welcome to this Self-Compassion meditation. Find a comfortable position — sitting or lying down. Let your body be supported. There is nothing you need to fix or achieve. Simply being here is enough.

When you’re ready, gently close your eyes, or soften your gaze downward.

[pause — 10s]

Notice your breath. In… and out. No need to control it. Just observe.

[pause — 20s]

Let your shoulders drop. Unclench your jaw. Allow your body to settle, as if sinking gently into the surface beneath you.

[pause — 15s]

Self-compassion is not self-indulgence. It is not weakness. It is the courage to meet yourself with honesty and kindness — especially on hard days.

[pause — 15s]

Place a hand over your heart, if that feels right. Feel its warmth. Its steady beat. Your heart has never stopped working for you.

[pause — 20s]

*May I be kind to myself in this moment.*

[pause — 15s]

*May I give myself the patience I would offer a good friend.*

[pause — 15s]

Bring to mind something that has felt difficult recently. A worry, a regret, a moment of shame or frustration. Let it be present without pushing it away.

[pause — 20s]

Notice where this lives in your body. Chest, belly, throat, shoulders. Breathe gently toward that area. Not to fix it — simply to acknowledge it.

[pause — 25s]

*This is a moment of suffering.*

[pause — 10s]

*Suffering is part of life.*

[pause — 10s]

*May I be kind to myself.*

[pause — 20s]

Now listen for the voice of your inner critic. It may say: *You should have done better. You’re not enough. Why did you do that?*

[pause — 15s]

Notice this voice without believing it completely. It often comes from a place of fear — trying to keep you safe by pushing you harder. You can thank it… and choose a different response.

[pause — 20s]

*Thank you for trying to protect me. But I choose kindness instead.*

[pause — 20s]

Imagine speaking to yourself as you would to a dear friend who is struggling. What would your kindest self say?

Perhaps: *You’re doing your best. This is hard, and that’s okay. You deserve care, not punishment.*

[pause — 25s]

Repeat softly, in your mind:

*May I forgive myself for being imperfect.*

[pause — 15s]

*May I learn and grow without harshness.*

[pause — 15s]

*May I treat myself with the same care I give to others.*

[pause — 25s]

Now a gentle body scan. Bring attention to the top of your head. Notice any sensation. Move down to your forehead, your eyes, your jaw. With each exhale, invite a little more softness.

[pause — 20s]

Shoulders and upper back — often where we hold responsibility and tension. Breathe into this area. *May I release what I cannot carry alone.*

[pause — 20s]

Chest and belly — the centre of emotion. Place both hands here if you wish. Feel the breath moving under your palms.

[pause — 25s]

Legs, feet, the ground beneath you. You are held. You are here. You are enough in this moment.

[pause — 25s]

Rest in stillness. If thoughts come, let them pass like clouds. Return again and again to: *May I be kind to myself.*

[pause — 40s]

*(Optional soft repeat during silence:)*  
*May I be kind to myself…*

[pause — 30s]

Begin to deepen your breath. Small movements in your fingers and toes. When you’re ready, gently open your eyes.

[pause — 10s]

You showed up today — not to perform, but to care for yourself. That matters.

Thank you for this practice. Carry this compassion with you into whatever comes next.

# Body Scan

---

## 5 minutes

Welcome to this Body Scan meditation. Find a comfortable position — lying down is ideal, but sitting works too. Let your body be fully supported. There is nowhere else you need to be.

Close your eyes when you’re ready.

[pause — 10s]

Take three slow breaths. Breathing in through your nose… and out through your mouth. With each exhale, allow yourself to arrive a little more fully in this moment.

[pause — 20s]

Bring your attention to your feet. Notice the soles, the toes, the tops of your feet. Any warmth, tingling, pressure, or nothing at all. There is no right way to feel. Simply notice.

[pause — 15s]

Move your attention up through your ankles and calves. Your knees. Your thighs. Without trying to change anything, observe what is here.

[pause — 15s]

Now your pelvis and lower belly. The area around your hips. Notice the breath moving the belly slightly — a gentle rise and fall.

[pause — 15s]

Bring awareness to your chest and upper back. The space around your heart. The rhythm of your breathing. Steady and quiet.

[pause — 15s]

Your hands and arms. From fingertips to shoulders. If you notice tension, you don’t need to force it away. Acknowledge it. Breathe.

[pause — 15s]

Your neck, jaw, and face. Soften the muscles around your eyes. Let your tongue rest gently. Unfurrow your brow.

[pause — 15s]

Now feel your body as a whole — from head to toe. One complete, breathing presence. Heavy. Relaxed. Here.

[pause — 20s]

Take one more deep breath in… and a long, slow breath out.

[pause — 10s]

When you’re ready, gently open your eyes. Notice how your body feels now compared to when you began.

Thank you for this practice.

## 10 minutes

Welcome to this Body Scan meditation. Find a comfortable position — lying down if you can, or sitting with support. Allow your body to settle. Close your eyes when you’re ready.

[pause — 10s]

Take three slow breaths. In through the nose… out through the mouth. With each exhale, feel yourself arriving here.

[pause — 20s]

We’ll move slowly through the body, region by region. There is nothing to fix. Your only task is to notice.

[pause — 10s]

**Feet.** Soles, toes, tops of the feet. Temperature. Contact with socks or the surface beneath you.

[pause — 20s]

**Ankles and lower legs.** Calves and shins. Any sensation — warmth, lightness, heaviness, or neutral.

[pause — 20s]

**Knees and thighs.** The large muscles of your upper legs. Let them feel supported. Nothing to hold.

[pause — 20s]

**Pelvis and hips.** The weight of your body resting here. Stable. Grounded.

[pause — 20s]

**Lower belly.** Notice the breath moving this area. Gentle expansion on the inhale. Soft release on the exhale.

[pause — 20s]

**Chest and upper back.** The space around your lungs and heart. Feel the breath filling this area, then leaving.

[pause — 20s]

**Hands and fingers.** Both hands. Palms, backs of the hands, each finger. Often we carry tension here without noticing.

[pause — 20s]

**Arms and shoulders.** Upper arms, elbows, forearms. Then the shoulders — a common place for stress to gather. Breathe into your shoulders. Imagine warmth softening them on each out-breath.

[pause — 25s]

**Neck and throat.** Be especially gentle here. Small, subtle sensations.

[pause — 15s]

**Face and head.** Jaw, cheeks, eyes, forehead, scalp. Soften each area. Let your whole head feel heavy and relaxed.

[pause — 25s]

Now expand your awareness to include your entire body at once. From the crown of your head to the tips of your toes. One breathing, living whole.

[pause — 30s]

Notice the quality of rest in your body now. Perhaps a little more ease. A little more space. However you feel is valid.

[pause — 20s]

Begin to deepen your breath. Gentle movement in your fingers and toes.

[pause — 10s]

When you’re ready, open your eyes slowly. Take a moment before returning to your day.

Thank you for giving your body this attention.

## 15 minutes

Welcome to this Body Scan meditation. Lie down if you can, or sit comfortably with your back supported. Adjust anything that needs adjusting — clothing, posture, temperature. Give yourself permission to fully arrive.

Close your eyes.

[pause — 15s]

Three slow breaths. In… and out. With each exhale, let go of the day behind you and the tasks ahead. For these next minutes, there is only this body, and this breath.

[pause — 25s]

**Left foot.** Toes, sole, heel. Notice sensation without naming it good or bad.

[pause — 20s]

**Right foot.** The same gentle attention. Both feet together now — grounded, present.

[pause — 20s]

**Lower legs.** Shins, calves, ankles. If you sense tension, breathe toward it. Not to force it away — simply to meet it with awareness.

[pause — 25s]

**Knees and thighs.** The large muscles that carry you through each day. Acknowledge them silently: *Thank you.*

[pause — 25s]

**Pelvis and hips.** The centre of stability. Feel the weight of your body resting here. Supported.

[pause — 25s]

**Lower belly.** Place a hand here if you wish. Feel the breath under your palm. Natural. Unforced.

[pause — 25s]

**Upper belly and lower ribs.** Notice expansion on the inhale. Softening on the exhale.

[pause — 25s]

**Chest and upper back.** The area around your heart and lungs. Be tender here. This is where we feel much of life.

[pause — 25s]

**Mid and lower back.** An area that often holds stress. Send breath there. Imagine warmth on each exhale.

[pause — 25s]

**Left hand and arm.** Fingertips to shoulder. Heavy. Relaxed.

[pause — 20s]

**Right hand and arm.** The same. Both arms resting completely.

[pause — 20s]

**Shoulders.** Lift them slightly on an inhale… and let them drop on the exhale. Once more. And release.

[pause — 25s]

**Neck.** Front, sides, back. Slow, careful attention.

[pause — 20s]

**Jaw and face.** Let the jaw hang loose. Soften the eyes, the cheeks, the forehead.

[pause — 20s]

**Scalp and crown.** The top of your head. Feel complete.

[pause — 20s]

Now the whole body at once. Head to toe. A single field of sensation. Breathing.

[pause — 30s]

We’ll practice a gentle tension release. On your next inhale, gently curl your toes and make soft fists.

[pause — 5s]

Hold…

[pause — 5s]

Exhale and release everything. Let go completely.

[pause — 20s]

Once more — inhale, gently lift your shoulders toward your ears.

[pause — 5s]

Exhale, drop them down. Heavy. Loose.

[pause — 20s]

Inhale… scrunch your face gently.

[pause — 5s]

Exhale… smooth it all away.

[pause — 25s]

Return to natural breathing. Whole body soft. If you’re practicing before sleep, let yourself grow heavier with each breath. If it’s daytime, simply rest in this relaxed awareness.

[pause — 40s]

*(Quiet hold — optional soft cue:)*  
*Nothing to do. Nowhere to go. Just this breath… and this body… at rest.*

[pause — 35s]

Begin to deepen your breath. Small movements in fingers and toes. Roll your ankles gently if you like.

[pause — 15s]

When you’re ready, open your eyes. Take a moment before sitting up. Notice what has shifted.

Thank you for this practice. Your body asks for little — only your attention. You gave it that today.
